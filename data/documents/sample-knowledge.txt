Production RAG Knowledge Assistant

This knowledge assistant is designed to answer questions using local business or project documents.

Core idea:
- The system stores documents in a vector database after converting them into embeddings.
- When a user asks a question, the assistant finds the most relevant document chunks.
- It then uses those chunks to ground the answer and reduces hallucination.

Use cases:
- Internal knowledge base for a startup.
- HR and policy document assistant.
- Customer support Q&A over FAQs and product documents.
- Research assistant for PDFs, notes, and project reports.

Best practices:
- Keep documents clean and structured.
- Split large documents into meaningful chunks.
- Use metadata like source, date, and category.
- Add a human review loop for important decisions.
- Monitor answer quality with real user feedback.
